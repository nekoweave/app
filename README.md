# NekoWeave — Professional Background Music & Audio Ads Automation System

**NekoWeave** is a lightweight, reliable, and powerful desktop application designed for background music management and automated audio marketing campaigns in the HoReCa (hotels, restaurants, cafes) and retail sectors. 

Optimized for stable 24/7 operation on Windows 10 and Windows 11, it ensures the perfect sound environment for your business.

## 🔥 Key Features

*   📻 **Intelligent Player & Internet Radio:** Stream online radio, play local tracks, or build custom playlists. NekoWeave seamlessly integrates with licensed audio libraries, USB storage devices, or royalty-free databases (Creative Commons), completely eliminating copyright risks.
*   🚨 **Air Raid Alert Monitoring (Smart Alerts):** A built-in safety module connected directly to the `alerts.in.ua` API. Upon an active threat in your specified region or city, NekoWeave smoothly dims the background music to broadcast clear emergency voice notifications for your guests and staff.
*   📅 **Advanced Visual Scheduler:** An innovative, DAW-inspired interface for professional audio layout management. Schedule promotional ads, jingles, internal announcements, and music background changes with split-second precision across the days of the week.
*   ⚡ **Silence Detector & Backup Failover (Blackout Proof):** If your internet connection drops or an online radio stream freezes, the intelligent silence detector triggers instantly. NekoWeave automatically switches the broadcast to a pre-selected local backup playlist (e.g., from a USB flash drive or local drive), ensuring your venue never faces awkward silence.
*   🪶 **Minimal System Footprint:** Highly optimized media engine. It runs invisibly in the background on low-spec mini-PCs, nettops, and touchscreen POS terminals without impacting cash register or accounting software performance.
